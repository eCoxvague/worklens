What the numbers mean

Visits
Estimated visits to the WorkLens demo, using GoatCounter’s session deduplication of up to eight hours.
Demo used
A visitor changes a solution bit or downloads a report. Initial page loading and resetting the example do not trigger this event.
Report downloaded
A visitor requests the example’s JSON report.

Events are deduplicated within a page and by GoatCounter’s session logic. These figures estimate activity; they do not identify people or establish unique monthly active users. Event totals must not be added to visits as if they were additional users.

Measurement scope

Tracking begins with this integration. Earlier visits are not backfilled. Local previews, automated browser checks, and this explanation page are excluded. The dashboard does not distinguish an ordinary owner visit from another visitor; the owner should use the opt-out below. Blockers and privacy preferences can reduce the measured totals.

Data and your choices

WorkLens sends a fixed page name and the two named demo events to GoatCounter. It removes query strings and referrers from the analytics payload. Account details, wallets, solution vectors, and downloaded report contents are not sent. Browser, screen, location, referrer, and individual-pageview collection are disabled in our dashboard settings.

GoatCounter uses request information transiently to estimate sessions. It does not require visitor cookies or a persistent visitor ID. Our integration honors Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. An opt-out stores only a functional preference in your browser.
